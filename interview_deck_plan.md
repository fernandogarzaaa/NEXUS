# KPMG Nexus: Enterprise AI Gateway
## Presentation Strategy
- **Executive Summary:** Focus on the "Triad of Enterprise AI" (Security, Compliance, Efficiency).
- **The Engine:** Introduce CHIMERA Ultimate as a hybrid gateway that solves the "cloud dilemma" (privacy vs. performance).
- **The Demo (The "Wow" Factor):** Use the Nexus Sandbox to show PII redaction *in real-time*.
- **The ROI:** Demonstrate the "Cost Router" which allows companies to use small, local, free models for 90% of traffic, reserving expensive cloud tokens only for complex strategy.
